# BIZ-IT: Small Business AI Infrastructure

## 1. Project Mission & Business Goal
**Biz-IT** is an independent business and development project dedicated to providing small businesses with AI-optimized, automated infrastructure. Its primary goal is to productize high-end AI processing and development services—such as web development, automated booking systems, and management tools—into selectable, autonomous units. BIZ-IT bridges the gap between complex AI development and practical, scalable SMB needs through a proprietary automated processing pipeline.

## 2. Core Service Offerings (AI-Optimized)
BIZ-IT provides modular, automated development services designed for rapid deployment and immediate monetization impact:

*   **Automated Web Presence**: AI-generated, conversion-optimized web environments and marketing assets.
*   **Intelligent Booking & Management**: Automated scheduling, client management, and resource allocation infrastructure.
*   **Infrastructure-as-a-Product**: Specific AI components (agents, data processors, automated workflows) that small businesses can integrate autonomously.

## 3. Business & Monetization Synopsis
The project operates as a high-velocity sales and processing engine. 

*   **Current Status**: Product availability is under active internal discussion, with significant development underway. The system is currently approaching a "Small-Scale Saleable" MVP status.
*   **Monetization Strategy**: Revenue is generated through the sale of discrete AI infrastructure items and recurring automated processing services.
*   **Selectable Autonomy**: Customers select specific infrastructure modules which are then processed and deployed via the BIZ-IT automated engine.

## 4. Infrastructure & Pricing Strategy
Biz-IT components are priced in a **100% selectable and autonomous** fashion. 
- **Modular Pricing**: A dynamic engine calculates valuation based on data density, investigative depth, and asset volume.
- **Service Dashboard**: A centralized, tool-focused interface for the independent assembly of data-driven Truth Packages.
